# Product Context

## Product

Campus Job Tracker is a local-first desktop application for managing campus recruiting opportunities, application progress, and interview retrospectives.

## Users

One job seeker who repeatedly captures job descriptions, tracks several applications at once, and wants interview questions and answers organized for later review.

## Core Tasks

- Capture a job from a Markdown or Word document, or preview public job details from a URL.
- Keep unsubmitted opportunities separate from active applications.
- Track application milestones and the latest activity date.
- Open a first-round, second-round, third-round, or HR interview record from its application stage.
- Review saved interview sessions grouped by company, role, and round.
- Edit local copies of role-specific resumes and export them to Word or PDF.

## Product Constraints

- Personal data stays on the device by default.
- Imported or scraped details remain editable and require user confirmation before saving.
- Original imported resume files remain on this device; edited resume text is stored locally.
- Missing publication dates stay unknown; the application never invents them.
- The prototype demonstrates navigation and editing states without claiming to have a database or a live scraper.
